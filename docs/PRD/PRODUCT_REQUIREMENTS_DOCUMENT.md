# Trufocus CRM Product Requirements Document

## 1. PRODUCT VISION

### Vision
Trufocus CRM is a Photography Business Operating System built for modern studios. It combines customer relationship management, project operations, finance, creative workflow, and AI assistance into one unified platform.

### What Trufocus CRM is
Trufocus CRM is not a standalone sales tool. It is a studio operating system that manages the entire customer lifecycle from lead capture to delivery and business closure. It connects marketing, sales, production, finance, and creative delivery in a single experience.

### Long-term vision
Trufocus CRM will become the global SaaS platform for photography, videography, and creative event businesses. It will serve small teams through enterprise studios with multi-company, white-label, and branded experiences.

### How it differs from generic CRMs
- Built specifically for photography and creative service workflows
- Handles creative production phases such as RAW upload, AI culling, editing, gallery delivery, and album design
- Includes integrated finance, contracts, invoices, and payment workflows for photo studios
- Supports customer-facing portals and gallery experiences natively
- Designed around studio roles rather than generic sales roles
- Includes AI copilots for operations, finance, and creative support

## 5. COMPLETE BUSINESS WORKFLOW

### Lead
- Capture leads from websites, forms, social, referrals, and manual entry
- Create enquiry records with customer contact details and event requirements

### ↓ Enquiry
- Qualify enquiry and assign a sales or CRM owner
- Create a quotation or proposal based on packages and services
- Track enquiry status and follow-up tasks

### ↓ Quotation
- Build a studio-specific quote with pricing, add-ons, and payment terms
- Send the quote to the customer through portal or email
- Record customer feedback and changes

### ↓ Approval
- Customer approves a quotation and signs contract
- Studio verifies terms and confirms booking
- Update enquiry and project status to booked

### ↓ Work Order
- Generate a work order from the approved quote
- Plan shoot details, dates, team assignments, and customer expectations
- Define payment schedule, contract terms, and delivery milestones

### ↓ Payment
- Issue invoice or payment request to the customer
- Track deposits, milestones, and final payments
- Send reminders for pending or overdue payments

### ↓ Assignment
- Assign photographers, videographers, assistants, and editors
- Confirm team availability and schedule allocations
- Create task checklists and shoot instructions

### ↓ Shoot
- Execute the shoot according to assignment and schedule
- Capture metadata, location details, and customer notes
- Confirm completion and asset intake readiness

### ↓ RAW Upload
- Upload RAW files and footage into the production pipeline
- Associate assets with the work order and shoot event
- Track upload status and storage readiness

### ↓ AI Culling
- Run AI-assisted culling on uploaded content
- Detect blur, closed eyes, duplicates, and best shots
- Group images by face, scene, and delivery category

### ↓ Photo Correction
- Apply RAW corrections, exposure, color balance, and noise reduction
- Prepare assets for editing and album review
- Track version history and approval checkpoints

### ↓ Editing
- Perform creative editing, retouching, and styling
- Review drafts and revise per customer feedback
- Deliver final edited assets to gallery or selection workflow

### ↓ Gallery
- Publish finished assets to a secure gallery
- Provide customer access through portal or direct link
- Enable downloads, favorites, and review options

### ↓ Customer Selection
- Allow customers to choose favorites and select final images
- Track selection status and communicate next steps
- Sync selected assets to album design and print workflows

### ↓ Album Design
- Build album layouts using selected photos
- Preview designs with customers and iterate
- Collect album approvals before print production

### ↓ Approval
- Seek final approval for album, delivery, and print-ready assets
- Record customer sign-off in the work order
- Trigger delivery planning and fulfillment

### ↓ Printing
- Prepare print-ready files and packaging instructions
- Coordinate print vendors or in-house production
- Track printing status and quality control

### ↓ Delivery
- Deliver final gallery, album, prints, and digital files to the customer
- Confirm delivery acceptance and collect remaining payments if needed
- Document closure notes and final customer feedback

### ↓ Project Closed
- Mark the project as completed
- Archive work order and customer records for reporting
- Generate post-sale insights and follow-up tasks

## 9. AI CULLING

### Blur detection
- Detect images with motion blur or focus issues
- Flag low-quality shots for review

### Closed eyes
- Identify images with closed or blinking eyes
- Provide suggested replacements from the same set

### Duplicate detection
- Identify duplicate or near-duplicate frames
- Recommend the best frame in each cluster

### Best shot
- Recommend highest quality images based on technical and aesthetic signals
- Surface best shots for customer selection and album workflows

### Ratings
- Assign confidence scores to images
- Allow team or customer ratings to refine culling
- Support custom rating thresholds and filters

### Face grouping
- Group images by detected faces for efficient review
- Allow selection by subject or family member
- Support face-based album organization

### Scene grouping
- Group images by location, color, or event type
- Support smart selection of sequences and story arcs
- Enable faster editing and playlist creation
